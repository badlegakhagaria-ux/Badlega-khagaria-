About Badlega Khagaria

Badlega Khagaria is a community-driven movement working towards positive and meaningful change in Khagaria. We believe that change begins with awareness, responsibility and collective action.

Our Mission

🛡️ Safety & Awareness

Promoting awareness and supporting a safer environment, especially for girls and women.

🧹 Clean Khagaria

Encouraging cleanliness, civic responsibility and a healthier community.

🌱 Environment

Spreading environmental responsibility and encouraging sustainable community practices.

📚 Education & Youth

Encouraging education, awareness, skills and meaningful participation of young people.

🤝 Help & Service

Supporting people in need and encouraging a culture of kindness and community service.

📢 Responsible Voice

Raising responsible voices against issues and practices that negatively affect our community.
