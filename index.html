Our Mission

Badlega Khagaria is a community-driven movement working for positive and responsible change.

🛡️

Safety & Awareness

Promoting awareness around girls' and women's safety and encouraging a safer community for everyone.

🌱

Clean & Green Khagaria

Supporting cleanliness, environmental responsibility and better public spaces.

📢

Responsible Voice

Raising responsible voices against wrong practices and issues affecting our community.

📚

Education & Awareness

Encouraging education, knowledge and social awareness among young people and the wider community.

🤝

Helping People

Supporting people in need and creating a culture of compassion, cooperation and community service.

🚀

Youth Development

Giving young people opportunities to participate, contribute and create meaningful change.

What We Believe

Small responsible actions can create meaningful change.

01 • Responsibility

We take responsibility for our community and surroundings.

02 • Unity

Positive change becomes stronger when people work together.

03 • Awareness

Awareness is the first step towards meaningful action.

04 • Integrity

We believe in honest, respectful and responsible work.
